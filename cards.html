CENTRAL CARDS — FAQ_ [READ THIS BEFORE ASKING MR GOLDER]
How do students get a card?
Student cards are granted on merit — leadership in enrichments, expos, clubs, service, or standout contributions. Only a small number of students get cards per season.
Can staff get cards anytime?
Yes. Staff cards can be created anytime.
Who runs the project?
The project is run by Alex Rose and Cole Van De Poll.
Why is Elon Musk (or other non-Central people) in the set?
Some non-Central figures appear for fun/collectibility. This does not imply any relationship with the school.
How do I buy trading cards?
Use the Shop tab, fill in your name, class, and school email, then place your order.
What are the rarities and odds?
Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra Rare, Legendary. Packs are weighted, not guaranteed.
I got duplicates. Is that normal?
Yes. Packs can contain duplicates. You can trade with friends.
When will my order be ready?
Check the yellow estimate on the Shop:
  • Thu/Fri: By Next Week (Monday–Tuesday)
  • Mon–Wed: By This Week (Tomorrow–Friday)
Where’s the card checker?
We retired it. New seasons use internal indexing.
Can I suggest a new card?
Yes — use the Suggest a Card tab. Make a case (powers, impact). Merit is key.
